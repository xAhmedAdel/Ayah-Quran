## Privacy Policy — Ayah

Last updated: 2026-11-17

### Overview

Ayah ("the app") is an offline-first Quran reading and wallpaper application.
**The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

### Data collection

The app collects **no data**:

- **No personal information** is requested, collected, or stored.
- **No analytics** SDKs are included.
- **No advertising** SDKs are included.
- **No crash reporting** SDKs are included.
- **No network permissions** are declared. The app operates fully offline.
- **No tracking** of any kind is performed.

### Data stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

- **Quran text**: the full Uthmani text (6,236 verses) imported from a
  verified dataset into a local Room database. This is public-domain
  religious text, not user data.
- **Bookmarks**: verses you bookmark (stored locally in Room).
- **Reading position**: the last surah and ayah you read (stored locally).
- **Wallpaper history**: records of wallpapers you applied (stored locally).
- **Preferences**: your theme, wallpaper settings, and audio preferences
  (stored locally in DataStore).
- **Generated wallpapers**: rendered wallpaper images (cached locally).

All of this data remains on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it.
None of it is transmitted to any server or third party.

### Third-party content

The app includes the following third-party content, bundled locally:

- **Quran text**: Tanzil Project (Uthmani, v1.1), Creative Commons
  Attribution 3.0 — [tanzil.net](https://tanzil.net)
- **Amiri typeface**: SIL Open Font License 1.1
- **Juz/page layout data**: derived from the Madani mushaf via quran.com

These are static content files. They do not collect data or communicate
with external servers.

### Permissions

The app requests one permission:

- **SET_WALLPAPER**: required to set Quranic wallpapers on your Home and
  Lock screens. This is a normal permission (not dangerous) and is granted
  at install time. It does not collect data.

### Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect any data from anyone,
including children.
